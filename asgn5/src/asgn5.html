Rubric Breakdown

Basic Scene: The application initializes a PerspectiveCamera and a DirectionalLight. It incorporates four distinct primary geometries (BoxGeometry, CylinderGeometry, SphereGeometry, and ConeGeometry). Animation loops are implemented via trigonometric functions for the orbiting sun and swaying palm foliage.

Textured Shape: Texture mapping is applied successfully. The ground plane utilizes a repeated sand texture, and the spherical objects (coconuts) utilize an external map texture.

Custom Model: Two distinct custom 3D models (.glb format) were imported via GLTFLoader. The models correctly retain and render their baked-in textures and intrinsic materials.

Controls: Scene navigation is fully supported through OrbitControls, enabling dynamic panning, zooming, and rotational camera movement.

Lights: Three distinct light sources operate within the scene: a DirectionalLight (simulating primary solar illumination), an AmbientLight (providing environmental fill), and a SpotLight (functioning as the rotating lighthouse beam).

Skybox: A complete 6-sided cubemap skybox is implemented utilizing CubeTextureLoader, satisfying the environmental background requirement.

20 Shapes: The scene contains exactly 40 distinct primary shapes constructed programmatically (excluding imported custom models), significantly exceeding the minimum threshold of 20.
